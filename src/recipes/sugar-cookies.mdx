<Recipe>
1. Preheat the oven to 350. Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. Whisk **2 1/2 c flour**, **2 t baking powder**, and **3/4 t salt**.
3. In another bowl, beat **2 sticks butter** with **1 c sugar**.
4. Beat in **2 eggs** and **1 t vanilla**.
5. Beat in flour mixture.
6. Roll dough into balls,[^1] roll in sugar, and place on baking sheet. Flatten them some.[^2]
7. Bake 10 mins.
8. Cool 5 mins, then remove from baking sheet.
</Recipe>

<Notes>
Original recipe from [Handle the Heat](https://handletheheat.com/soft-chewy-sugar-cookies/).
</Notes>

<Footnotes>
[^1]: Make them pretty small. They spread out a lot.
[^2]: Bottom of a measuring cup works well.
</Footnotes>
