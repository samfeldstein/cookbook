<Recipe>
1. Heat some oil in a pan over medium.
2. Add **3 cloves of minced garlic** to the pan and saute until fragment, 2-3 mins.
3. Add **1/2 c coconut milk**, **1 t turmeric**, and **2 T chili paste**.
4. Bring to a boil, turn heat to low.
5. Stir in **1/2 c peanut butter**, **1 T soy sauce**, and **1/2 t sesame oil**.
</Recipe>
