<Recipe>
1. Oven 425
2. Slice chicken in half horizontally
3. Pound to an even thickness.
4. Spice as desired.
5. Bake 18-20 mins.
6. Let rest 5-10 minutes before slicing to allow the juices to redistribute.
</Recipe>
