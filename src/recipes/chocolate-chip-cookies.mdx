<Recipe>
1. Preheat oven to 375. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper and set aside.
2. Mix **1 1/2 c flour**, **1/2 tsp baking soda**, **1/4 tsp baking powder**, and **1/2 tsp salt**. Set aside.
3. Cream together **1/2 c softened salted butter**, **1/2 c sugar**, and **1/2 c packed light brown sugar**.
4. Beat in **1 egg** and **1 tsp vanilla** until light, about 1 minute.
5. Mix in the dry ingredients until combined.
6. Add **1 c chocolate chips** and mix well.
7. Roll **2–3 Tbsp** of dough at a time into balls and place evenly spaced on the prepared cookie sheets.
8. Bake 8–10 minutes, until just barely starting to turn brown.
9. Cool 5 minutes, then remove to cooling rack.
</Recipe>

<Notes>
Halved from original. Makes about a dozen cookies.

Original recipe from [Joy Food Sunshine](https://joyfoodsunshine.com/the-most-amazing-chocolate-chip-cookies/).
</Notes>
